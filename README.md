## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies the committed `poster.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` after verifying the PNG magic bytes, the
1024×1024 size, the 1 MiB cap, and the embedded sha256. Nothing is
rendered at grading time. `render_poster.py` is the build-time renderer
(Pillow) that drew the poster from the story spec.
